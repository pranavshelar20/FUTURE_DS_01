# FUTURE_DS_01 — Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 📊 Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics

### Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset using Power BI to understand business sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, customer segments, and monthly sales trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and identify:

- Revenue trends over time
- Top-selling products
- High-performing categories
- Regional sales and profit performance
- Customer segment performance
- Business opportunities and areas for improvement

The analysis is presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and calculations
- **Microsoft Excel/CSV** — Source dataset
- **GitHub** — Project documentation and submission

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared using Power Query.

The following steps were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Checked for duplicate records
- Corrected Order Date and Ship Date data types
- Validated numerical fields
- Checked category, region, segment, and shipping-mode values
- Created a Date Table for time-based analysis
- Created relationships between the Date Table and sales data

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology is the leading category

Technology generates approximately **$0.84M in sales**, making it the highest-selling category.

### 2. West is the strongest region

The West region generates approximately **$0.73M in sales** and also has the highest regional profit at approximately **$105K**.

### 3. Consumer is the largest customer segment

The Consumer segment contributes approximately **$1.16M in sales**, making it the largest revenue-generating segment.

### 4. Sales increase toward the end of the year

The monthly sales trend shows stronger performance around **September and November–December**, indicating potential seasonal demand.

### 5. Top-selling product

The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** is the highest-selling product in the Top 10 Products analysis, generating approximately **$62K in sales**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-performing Technology products.
- Investigate the reasons for lower sales performance in the South region.
- Strengthen customer retention strategies for the Consumer segment.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns before the stronger year-end sales period.
- Evaluate products and regions using both sales and profit rather than revenue alone.
